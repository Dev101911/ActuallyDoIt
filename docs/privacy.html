Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ActuallyDoIt app ("the App") handles your information. Your privacy matters, and the App is designed to keep your data on your device.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not have any servers, accounts, or analytics. We do not gather, transmit, sell, or share any information about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

All content you create in the App — including your tasks, tags, schedules, and nudge preferences — is stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device except through features you control, such as Apple's own device backup and iCloud sync (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy).

Notifications and Live Activities

The App uses local notifications and Live Activities to nudge you about your tasks. These are scheduled and displayed entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to us or any third party.

Purchases (Tip Jar)

The App offers optional tips through Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). All transactions are handled by Apple. We never receive or store your payment details. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple processes purchases.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at deadpan-pest-9g@icloud.com.
